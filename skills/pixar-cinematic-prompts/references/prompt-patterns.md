# Prompt Patterns

## Basic Silent Family Scene

Ultra-realistic Pixar-style 3D animation, vertical 9:16, silent no dialogue, [character description with face lock], [emotion], [setting], golden hour soft warm light, gentle particles, slow push-in camera, natural expressive faces, identical facial features across frames, soft fabric details, warm emotional tone.

## Character Consistency Block (copy-paste)

exact same facial structure, eye spacing, nose shape, jawline, skin texture and undertone, hair/hijab style and color, identical proportions and identity across all frames and shots, no face drift

## 3-Shot Hero Product Workflow

1. Wide reveal: product in context, slow push-in, magical soft particles.  
2. Detail close: material and transformation fidelity, photo-accurate.  
3. Emotional close: user reaction or lifestyle, consistent character, warm light.

Always specify: vertical 9:16, silent or specific audio, consistency locks.

## Advanced Lighting Examples

### Golden hour rim + warm fill
soft golden hour sunlight from behind and side, strong gentle rim light outlining hair and shoulders, warm fill from front, long soft shadows, floating dust particles catching the light, vertical 9:16, silent

### Rembrandt indoor portrait
Rembrandt lighting, key light 45 degrees creating soft triangle on the far cheek, gentle fill, warm practical lamp in background, cinematic depth of field, identical facial features, vertical 9:16, silent

### Volumetric window light
soft volumetric light beams streaming through a window, haze and particles catching the rays, cool-warm contrast, subject gently illuminated, ethereal yet natural, slow dolly, vertical 9:16, silent

### Blue hour with warm practicals
blue hour twilight ambient light, warm glowing lamps and window light as key, soft rim from sky, intimate mood, gentle particles, identical character, vertical 9:16, silent

### Beauty butterfly for product/character close
butterfly lighting, soft key light from above and slightly front, minimal under-nose shadow, even flattering skin with subsurface scattering feel, clean highlights, vertical 9:16, silent

### Split lighting for emotion
soft split lighting, one side of face warmly lit, other side in gentle shadow, high emotional contrast but soft edges, motivated by window, identical face lock, vertical 9:16, silent
