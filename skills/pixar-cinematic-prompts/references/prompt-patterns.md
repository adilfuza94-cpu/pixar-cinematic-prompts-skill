# Prompt Patterns

## Basic Silent Family Scene

Ultra-realistic Pixar-style 3D animation, vertical 9:16, silent no dialogue, [character description with face lock], [emotion], [setting], golden hour soft warm light, gentle particles, slow push-in camera, natural expressive faces, identical facial features across frames, soft fabric details, warm emotional tone.

## Character Consistency Block (copy-paste)

exact same facial structure, eye spacing, nose shape, jawline, skin texture and undertone, hair/hijab style and color, identical proportions and identity across all frames and shots, no face drift

## 3-Shot Hero Product Workflow

1. Wide reveal: product in context, slow push-in, magical soft particles.  
2. Detail close: material and transformation fidelity, photo-accurate.  
3. Emotional close: user reaction or lifestyle, consistent character, warm light.

Always specify: vertical 9:16, silent or specific audio, consistency locks.
